Table Keeper Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Table Keeper is a free tool for running Dungeons & Dragons games. It is built to keep your data on your own device. There are no accounts, no adverts and no analytics.

What the app stores

Your campaigns, characters, monsters and notes are saved only in your device's browser storage. We never receive them. If you clear the app's data or uninstall it, they are deleted.

Live tables

When a DM starts a live table, other players can join with a 6-character code. Party details (names, stats, HP, conditions, spell slots, items and the initiative order) are then sent directly between the devices at the table.

  • To connect, devices use the free PeerJS connection service (peerjs.com) and public STUN servers. These see your device's IP address and the table code while making the connection. They don't receive your game data.
  • Monster HP, AC and attacks stay on the DM's device.
  • Anyone with the table code can view and edit the party while the table is live. Only share the code with people at your game.

Other services

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts and the live-table library from unpkg.com. Like any website, these services receive your IP address when you download those files. We don't share any other information with them.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
